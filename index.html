Artist Statement

“Art is a way of thinking. It expresses what words cannot.”

— Janakiraman

Through watercolour, I explore the quiet beauty of everyday life, drawing inspiration from the streets of Pondicherry, the landscapes around Auroville, and the timeless rhythm of South India.

Every painting is an attempt to capture not only a place, but also a feeling. I hope these works invite the viewer to pause, observe, and discover beauty in moments that often pass unnoticed.
